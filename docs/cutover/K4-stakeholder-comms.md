# Stakeholder communication pack (K4)

Send ≥48 hours before go-live, with the exact window and impact. Replace [brackets].

## K4.1 — Email to parents/guardians

**Subject:** JNTUCEJ Gate Monitor goes live on [DATE] — what changes for you

Dear Parent/Guardian,

From [DATE], JNTUCEJ will use a new campus gate-monitoring system for your child's entry and exit. You will receive an SMS when your child leaves campus for home, and when they return. You do not need to do anything to receive these messages — they go to the mobile number the college already has on file.

**What changes for you:**
- More visibility. You will know when your child exits and returns.
- Pass requests online. Leave and home-out requests can be submitted and tracked, instead of paper.
- One number to call. The campus security line is [PHONE], available 24/7.

**What does not change:** your child's attendance, fees, or hostel arrangements.

If you believe the mobile number we have is wrong, or you do not want SMS notifications, contact [EMAIL] or [PHONE] before [DATE]. You can withdraw consent for notifications at any time.

Privacy notice (English and Telugu): [URL]

— Principal, JNTUCEJ

## K4.2 — Announcement to students

**Subject:** Your new campus ID and gate pass system — read this before Monday

From [DATE], your college ID QR code is how you enter and exit campus. Bring your ID card. Every day. No ID, no scan.

**What you do:**
1. Open the student app / portal at [URL].
2. Check your photo and roll number are correct. If not, raise it at [office] before [DATE].
3. Your QR code is in ID → Show QR.

**What's new:**
- Leave requests: Leave → Request. You'll see each approval stage and who has it.
- Mess: choose veg/non-veg by [TIME] the day before. Miss the cutoff and you eat what's served.
- Notifications: your parents will get an SMS when you leave and return. That is by design.

**What to do if your QR doesn't scan:** go to the gate operator. They will check you in manually. This is not a punishment — it's a fallback so you're never blocked.

## K4.3 — Memo to faculty and staff

**Subject:** Gate Monitor — what changes for you on [DATE]

From [DATE], all campus entry/exit goes through the new gate system. Your staff ID is your QR code.

**For faculty:**
- Attendance marking is unchanged on the surface — you still mark your class. The system now records your own gate entry automatically.
- Approvals (leave, permissions) move from paper to the portal. You'll see a queue at [URL]. Approve or reject within the SLA shown.
- If you're a class teacher, your class roster and today's attendance are one click from My Classes.

**For staff:**
- Your daily attendance is now automatic from gate scans. No separate register.
- Leave requests: Requests → New. Your HOD or supervisor sees it immediately.

Training: [DATE] at [TIME] in [ROOM]. Bring your laptop. [N] seats, [N] already filled.
