# Gate Monitor Administrator Guide

## 1. System Administration Overview
As an Administrator or System Admin, you have full visibility and governance over campus gate operations, compliance, security, and integrations.

## 2. Key Operational Modules
- **User & Role Management** (`/admin/users`, `/admin/staff`, `/admin/students`): Create, update, assign roles, and approve role escalation requests.
- **Gate Schedules & Curfew Engine** (`/admin/gates/schedule`): Define access window rules (night curfews, exam hours) and holiday overrides.
- **Announcements & Broadcasts** (`/admin/announcements`): Broadcast push banners to specific user roles or full campus audience.
- **Support Triage Desk** (`/admin/support`): Manage, assign, and resolve user support tickets.
- **Advanced Analytics & Reports** (`/admin/analytics/advanced`): Execute custom queries, view occupancy trends, and export CSV reports.
- **Integrations Hub** (`/sysadmin/integrations`): Configure HR sync, SIS sync, SMS/Email gateways, and turnstile controllers.
- **Compliance & Retention** (`/sysadmin/compliance`): Run retention policy sweeps and perform PII anonymization / GDPR exports.
- **Security & Alerting** (`/sysadmin/alerts`, `/sysadmin/security`): Configure Prometheus alert rules, monitor failed logins, manage IP allowlists.
