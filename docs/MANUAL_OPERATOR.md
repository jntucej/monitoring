# Gate Operator User Manual
## Unified Campus Access Management System

---

## 1. Introduction

As a Gate Operator, you are the first point of contact for everyone entering and exiting the campus.

**Your Responsibilities:**
- Scan all persons entering/exiting campus
- Verify identity using photo verification
- Handle exceptions and edge cases
- Ensure smooth flow during peak hours

---

## 2. Scanning Operations

1. **Person approaches gate**
2. **Open Scanner** (click SCAN QR or trigger camera)
3. **Position QR Code**
4. **Review Person Details & Person Type Badge** (`Student`, `Faculty`, `Staff`, `Worker`, `Visitor`, `Parent`)
5. **Photo Verification**
6. **Select Direction (ENTRY / EXIT)**
7. **Confirm Scan**

---

## 3. Person Type Behaviors

| Type | Entry | Exit | Special Notes |
|------|-------|------|---------------|
| **Student** | Free | Pass required for hostel students | Check hostel curfew |
| **Faculty** | Free | Free | Auto-log, no pass needed |
| **Staff** | Free | Free | Auto-log, no pass needed |
| **Worker** | Free (within hours) | Free (within hours) | Restricted 6AM-10PM |
| **Visitor** | Check-in required | Check-out required | Must have valid pass |
| **Parent** | Free | Free | Linked to student(s) |
