Department of CSE

Computer Science & Engineering

The CSE department is the heart of technological innovation at JNTUHCEJ, offering a rigorous curriculum in computing, algorithms, and software engineering.

Accredited by the NBA, the department prepares students for careers in software development, AI, data science, and research. With state-of-the-art labs and experienced faculty, students gain hands-on experience in cutting-edge technologies.

Department of ECE

Electronics & Communication Engineering

The ECE department bridges the physical and digital worlds, covering electronics, communication systems, signal processing, and embedded systems.

With a focus on VLSI design, IoT, and wireless communication, the department equips students with skills for the rapidly advancing electronics industry. The M.Tech program in DSCE offers advanced specialization.

Department of EEE

Electrical & Electronics Engineering

The EEE department is a powerhouse of energy systems, power electronics, and electrical machine expertise — accredited by the NBA.

Students delve into power generation, transmission, renewable energy, and smart grid technologies. The M.Tech program in Electrical Power Systems (EPS) offers advanced specialization in this critical field.

Department of ME

Mechanical Engineering

The ME department is the backbone of manufacturing and design innovation, accredited by the NBA for program excellence.

Covering thermodynamics, mechanics, CAD/CAM, robotics, and materials science, the department prepares engineers for automotive, aerospace, and manufacturing industries. The M.Tech program in Engineering Design offers advanced specialization.
