Admission Process

How to Apply

A simple, transparent process to secure your seat at JNTUHCEJ.

📝

1. Apply

Submit your application through the TS EAMCET / TS PGECET counseling process.

📋

2. Document Verification

Verify your academic records, certificates, and eligibility documents.

✅

3. Seat Allotment

Receive your seat allotment based on rank and preference.

🎓

4. Enroll

Complete fee payment and join the JNTUHCEJ family.

Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria

B.Tech (UG Programs)

  • ✓ Passed Intermediate (10+2) with Mathematics, Physics, and Chemistry.
  • ✓ Qualified in TS EAMCET examination.
  • ✓ Lateral entry via TS ECET for direct 2nd year admission (6 seats per branch).
  • ✓ Minimum qualifying marks as per TS EAMCET norms.

M.Tech (PG Programs)

  • ✓ B.Tech / BE degree in the relevant branch of engineering.
  • ✓ Qualified in TS PGECET / GATE examination.
  • ✓ Minimum 50% aggregate marks (45% for reserved categories).
  • ✓ Specializations: CSE, IT, DSCE, EPS, Engineering Design.
