About Us

Gateway to Excellence in Engineering Education

Established in 2007 as a constituent college of Jawaharlal Nehru Technological University Hyderabad, JNTUHCEJ operates autonomously with a coveted NAAC 'A+' Grade accreditation. Recognized by UGC under Section 2(f) and 12(B), the institution is committed to academic rigor and holistic development.
